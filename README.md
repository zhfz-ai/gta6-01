Solmara: Sunset Heist is a 3D open-world action game set in Solmara, a fictional sun-soaked coastal city of downtown towers, a beach boulevard, a marina, hillside homes, and an industrial port. You play Dario Cole, an ex-getaway driver back in town to pay off his late brother's debt to club owner Vera Solano. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel, steal and drive cars through live traffic and crowds of pedestrians, outrun police chases as your wanted level climbs, and work through a five-mission story — from a tutorial getaway to a sunset finale — with taxi fares, stunt jumps, and collectible sun postcards on the side. Built with three.js, with a full HUD, circular minimap, fist and pistol combat, cash progression, and a localStorage save.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080
