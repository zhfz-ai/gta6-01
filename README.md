**Solmara: Sunset Heist** — a 3D open-world action game set in Solmara, a sun-soaked fictional coastal city. Play Dario Cole, an ex-getaway driver back in town to pay off his late brother's debt to club owner Vera Solano. Explore downtown towers, the beach boulevard, the marina, and hillside homes on foot or behind the wheel: steal any car, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs, and work through a five-mission story chain — from a tutorial getaway to a three-star sunset chase — plus taxi fares, stunt jumps, and collectible sun postcards. Traffic, pedestrians, fist and pistol combat, a full HUD with minimap, and a localStorage save round out the world. Built with three.js, all procedural, no network requests at runtime.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080
